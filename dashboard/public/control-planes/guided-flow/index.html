⚠️ Candidate Queue Clear: Blast Radius Impact

Clearing the candidate queue causes broad side-effects across admission state. The following resources will be purged or reset:

  • 1. All Candidate Ideas: All unpinned and pending backlog items are removed.
  • 2. Active Objective Pin: The currently pinned objective is cleared.
  • 3. Next-Run Request: Any pending tick admission flag is reset to false.
  • 4. Linked Steering Directives: Queue-derived runtime steering instructions are cancelled.
