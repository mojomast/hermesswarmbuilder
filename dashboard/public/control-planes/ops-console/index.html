⚠️ Clear Queue Blast-Radius Impact Preview

Clearing the candidate ideas queue carries system-wide operational consequences. The following resources and intents will be permanently revoked and purged:

  • All Queued Candidate Items: All pending unpinned and pinned items in SQLite storage.
  • Pinned Active Candidate: The current priority pin lock will be released.
  • Current Swarm Objective: Active runner objective pointer will be reset to Idle.
  • Pending Next-Run Request: Any scheduled one-shot ticket will be cancelled.
  • Requested-Run-Now Flag: Active admission trigger will be revoked.
  • Queue-Linked Steering Directives: Directives bound to candidate queue items will expire.
This operation requires Administrator role permissions and creates an irreversible audit record.
