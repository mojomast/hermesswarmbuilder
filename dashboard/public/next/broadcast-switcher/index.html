System Overview: Modeled after television Master Control Room production switchers (Grass Valley Kayenne & Blackmagic ATEM 4 M/E), following SMPTE ST 2110 IP broadcast standards.

Crosspoint Buses & Transitions

  • PGM Bus (Red): On-air video feed transmitted to output.
  • PVW Bus (Green): Cued preview source ready for transition.
  • T-Bar Fader: Drag handle for manual dissolve/mix transition between PVW and PGM.
  • Downstream Keyers (DSK): Injects live specification and plan overlays onto the program feed.
