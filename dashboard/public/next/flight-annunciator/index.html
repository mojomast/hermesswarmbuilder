System Overview: Modeled after modern commercial aerospace cockpits (Boeing 777/787 and Airbus A350), adhering to FAA AC 25.1322-1 and the "Dark Cockpit" philosophy.

Glareshield & Annunciator Logic

  • MASTER WARNING (Red): Flashes on critical workflow blocks and hard execution halts.
  • MASTER CAUTION (Amber): Flashes on non-blocking warnings or deblock requests.
  • Korry Split-Legend Switches: Upper legend indicates subsystem identity; lower legend displays active state.
  • Electronic Checklist (ECL): Step-by-step verification of flight gates and requirements.
