        Navigation and interpretation
        Press T to physically minimize or restore the command folio. Minimized mode removes its full plane and backing, exposes the archive, and uses a wider camera envelope. Its small labelled restore plinth remains in the scene. Arrow keys select labelled loci and Enter opens the selected context. A first pointer or touch activation selects a locus without restoring the folio; activate it again when full detail is needed.
        In the folio, arrow keys move among controls and Enter activates. W, A, S, and D orbit; wheel, pinch, plus, or minus zoom. Tab is never trapped and leaves the canvas. Press 0 to reset the current camera envelope, M to switch modes, and ? for scene help.
        The fixed 16:9 physical folio cannot remain readable in a narrow portrait viewport. At a height-to-width ratio of 1.15 or greater, this complete semantic mode is automatic; rotating below that threshold restores 3D unless semantic mode was selected manually. Mobile landscape remains in 3D.
        The circular archive is layout, not an operational measurement. Runs, agents, tools, plans, gates, queue items, iterations, events, and audit records are server evidence. Red means reported blocker or failure evidence, not a prediction.
        Reduced-motion changes are applied at runtime. Rendering is invalidation-driven and sleeps while hidden. WebGL creation, shader failure, and context loss expose this semantic surface; restoration permits returning to the archive.
